What's inside ውስጡ ምን አለ?

  • All 34 letters, each with a learning page and a practice page
  • All 7 sounds of every letter to trace and write
  • A picture word for every letter in Amharic and English
  • A full fidel chart with pronunciation
  • Ge'ez numbers 1–10, colours, family and kind words
  • 4 game pages with an answer key
  • A Fidel Journey tracker and a Fidel Champion certificate
  • A QR code to hear the sounds on YouTube
